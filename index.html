Designing experiences
through play.

I'm an independent photographer, writer, artistic researcher, and art curator who designs experiences through play and genuine connection with people and the environment surrounding them. I hold an Erasmus Mundus Joint Master Degree in Media Arts Cultures from Aalborg University, and continue to delve into the intersection of arts and technology as an online creative resident at Europeana. Currently based in Aarhus, Denmark, I bring creativity to life through play as a Play Agent at LEGO House's MINI Chef in Billund.
